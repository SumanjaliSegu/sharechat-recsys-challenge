VALIDATION

A holdout that behaves like the test days

Time split

Train before 27 Oct 18:00, score the last 24 hours. Every number in this talk is on that unseen day.

Test-mix check

Re-weight the holdout to the test’s 16% share of new videos. A change must win on both checks.

A strict bar

Keep a change only at +0.0002 on both, the size of seed noise.

Result: holdout → public dropped by the same ~0.0065 for every model family, so the holdout ranks models the way the test does.

The uniform drop matters: it tells us no family is secretly better on the later days, so the blend weights chosen on holdout are safe for the private leaderboard.
