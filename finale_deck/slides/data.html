THE DATA

Rich rows, but no persistent user

User and post vectors

7 user embeddings and 7 post embeddings of 32 dimensions each. We turned them into 49 dot and cosine match scores.

ShareChat’s own history

~450 numeric columns, including history watch rates and user–post affinity, plus 12 low-cardinality categories.

New every session

User vectors change per session; 93% of test users are new. Test also has 16% never-seen videos.

Key finding from data exploration: there is no stable user id to memorise. That is why the approach learns general watch patterns from embeddings and history rates rather than per-user memory.
