The serving-time rule: each row is predicted on its own, from that row’s features only. No information from other rows in the same request.

This rule shaped everything. In production the model sees one impression at a time, so anything that needs to look at the other rows in a request is not allowed. A lot of easy signal sits exactly there, and we left it out on purpose.
