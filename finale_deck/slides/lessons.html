LESSONS

What we would tell the next team

Trust a strict bar

Gains under seed noise did not survive the public board, twice. A +0.0002 bar on two checks saved us from chasing noise.

Diversity beats tuning

New kinds of networks moved the blend; more tuning of the same trees did not.

Context at serving time

Request context is worth ~0.05 AUC. Logging a feed-slot feature at impression time would be the largest real gain.

The last point is a concrete product suggestion for ShareChat: if the feed position were logged with each impression, it would be legal at serving time and very predictive.
